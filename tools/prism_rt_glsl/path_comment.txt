tiles: the anchor's regions (8x8 tiles' sums of CE's light and the traced
light) and the exposure's meter (their histograms).

exposure: display.prism_exposure's exposure for the frame, from the meter.

atrous: the spatial half (three edge-avoiding a-trous passes), the last
writing what the surfaces read (xgpu.h's XGPU_AMBIENT_OCCLUSION_RT_LIGHT):
